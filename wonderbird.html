How to play

  1. Launch. Drag your pilot back on the slingshot and let go. (Keyboard: hold Space, aim with ↑ ↓, let go.)
  2. Fly. Tap, click or press Space to flap through the gaps in the sandstone.
  3. Collect. Gold coins 25, blue gems 150. The black-and-gold Mr. Fixit badge flies you through on autopilot for a few seconds.
  4. Score. Clear the gates to finish a level. Fewer flaps score more: glide for a bonus. 99 levels, each harder than the last, from sunrise to midnight.

The rules

  • One play a day, with 3 lives. You can play once a day. Each crash costs a life; when all 3 are gone, your play for the day is over and you go back to the MBS website. You’re welcome to play again tomorrow (it resets at midnight, Sydney time).
  • One person, one play. One phone number or one email is one person: another entry with the same phone or email shares the same daily play.
  • Your best play counts. Your best play of the week (Monday to Sunday) goes on the leaderboard.
  • Fair play. Every score is checked by our server. Extra entries, shared accounts or tricks to get more plays count as cheating and are removed.

🏆 Top 3 scores each week (Monday to Sunday) win MBS vouchers: 🥇 $50 · 🥈 $30 · 🥉 $20.
