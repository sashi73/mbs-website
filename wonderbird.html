How to play

  1. Launch. Drag the bird back on the slingshot and let go. (Keyboard: hold Space, aim with ↑ ↓, let go.)
  2. Fly. Tap, click or press Space to flap through the gaps in the sandstone.
  3. Collect. Gold coins 25, blue gems 150. The black-and-gold Mr. Fixit badge flies you through on autopilot for a few seconds.
  4. Score. Clear the gates to finish a level. Fewer flaps score more: glide for a bonus. 99 levels, and 3 lives a run.
