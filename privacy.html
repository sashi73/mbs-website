Privacy

Your data, handled with care.

Global Knowledge House (ABN 38 107 769 345) runs GKH Project Champ. This policy explains what we collect and why, under the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

Sign-in details (username, or your Google account name and email if you use Sign in with Google), the project information you and your team enter (projects, tasks, people, costs, risks, documents), and a record of actions taken in the console.

When you chat with Champ

Champ, our assistant, may ask for your preferred language, name, phone number, email address and whether you would like a welcome package. Every question is optional: tap Skip or just ask your question. We keep what you choose to share so GKH can follow up with you and send the welcome package if you ask for it. Tell Champ “forget me” at any time to delete your details, or email us. Your phone number and email address are never sent to the AI service.

How we use it

Only to run Project Champ for you: to sign you in, store and show your projects, produce reports, and keep the service secure. When you use an AI feature, the relevant project details are sent to our AI service provider to produce the answer; contact details of stakeholders are not sent.

Who we share it with

Our hosting provider (Netlify), Google (only to check a Google sign-in) and our AI service provider. We do not sell your information.

Keeping it safe

Passwords are stored hashed, sessions are signed, and access is limited by role. You can ask us to correct or delete your information at any time.

Contact

Email ceo@gkhconsultancy.com. If you are not happy with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
