Privacy policy

How Modern Building Solutions Pty Ltd (ABN 50 159 309 181) collects, uses and looks after your personal information. Last updated 3 November 2026.

Who we are

Modern Building Solutions Pty Ltd ("MBS", "we") is a licensed builder (licence 267071C) in Sydney, New South Wales. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What we collect

  • When you ask for a quote or contact us (by phone, the contact form or the Mr. Fixit chat): your name, phone number, email address, suburb or address, the work you need, and any photos you send.
  • When we do work for you: the details needed to quote, plan, carry out and invoice the job, such as site details, quotes, invoices and payments.
  • When you sign in to our staff console (staff, contractors and invited users only): your username or, if you sign in with Google, your Google account's name and email address. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Basic website data: a simple visit count. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

  • To answer your enquiry, prepare quotes, plan and carry out the work, and send invoices.
  • To contact you about your job.
  • To run our business, keep records we must keep by law, and keep our systems secure.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

Only as needed to do the work and run our systems:

  • Sub-contractors and suppliers working on your job, with the details they need.
  • The service providers that run our website and systems: website hosting and data storage (Netlify), email delivery, staff notifications (Telegram), and an AI service that helps answer chat questions and prepare documents. They may store or process information outside Australia.
  • Government bodies or others where the law requires it.

Signing in with Google

If you choose "Sign in with Google" on our staff console, Google tells us your name and email address so we can let you in. We use them only to give you access and to keep a record of who signed in. You can remove our access at any time in your Google account settings (Security, Third-party connections).

Wonder Bird weekly voucher draw

When you enter our Wonder Bird game draw (the "🎁 Win $50" link), we collect your first and last name, contact number and email, and the scores you play. We use them to run the weekly leaderboard, to contact winners, to issue and check vouchers, and to tell you about MBS offers. You can ask us to stop or to delete your entry at any time.

  • Each week runs Monday to Sunday, Sydney time. Your best score in the week counts.
  • The top 3 scores win an MBS discount voucher: 1st $50, 2nd $30, 3rd $20. One voucher per person per week.
  • We contact winners by email or phone after the week ends. Each voucher has its own number.
  • Vouchers come off the price of MBS work. They are valid for 90 days from issue, can be used once, and cannot be swapped for cash.
  • Entries with false details, or scores made by cheating, are removed. MBS's decision is final.

Keeping it safe

Our systems use secure (HTTPS) connections, sign-in with access levels, and passwords stored in encrypted form. We keep information only as long as we need it for the job, our records and the law.

Your choices

You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it where we don't need to keep it. Contact us and we will reply within 30 days.

Contact and complaints

Email mbs.pty.ltd@gmail.com or call 0420 494 990. If you are not happy with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
