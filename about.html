About MBS

Quality work.
Second to none.

Modern Building Solutions delivers full building and construction services: new buildings, extensions, renovations, remedial work, painting, waterproofing and heritage renovation, for homes, strata, commercial and government properties.

We are a family business, building across Sydney since 2012. Every site is run to European standards of management and quality, with modern customer service and a team that is proud of its work. Our goal is simple: raise the standard of renovation and remedial work, using high-quality materials, methods and technology.
