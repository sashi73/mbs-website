Project Champ is built by Global Knowledge House (GKH), a boutique consultancy that has spent more than a decade running national programs, strategy and transformation work for governments and businesses.

Who we are

Global Knowledge House

GKH is a boutique consulting firm with offices in Sydney and Kuala Lumpur. Since 2010 we have delivered consultancy projects in 8 countries, and with our affiliate partners we reach clients in 14 countries across Asia, the Middle East and Africa.

We work with everyone from government ministries to medium-sized private businesses, on technology, ESG and sustainability, strategy, and program and project management. Our consultants hold doctorates and MBAs, have more than 10 years of consulting experience each, and have served as director general, chief advisor, CEO and lead scientist. They publish white papers and speak at international forums.

Project Champ grew out of that work: the planning, governance and reporting discipline we use on national programs, made simple enough for any project team.
