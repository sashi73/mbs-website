MBS JOB TRACKER (Telegram bot) - setup, once, about 5 minutes
==============================================================
Everything runs on Netlify. Jobs, reminders and photos are stored in Netlify Blobs (free, nothing to sign up for).
Use the same Telegram bot that already sends you the website enquiries, and your Mr.FixitB channel.

0. Mr.FixitB channel (skip if the enquiry alerts already arrive in it):
   - Telegram > Mr.FixitB > channel name at the top > Administrators > Add Admin > your bot.
     Turn ON "Post messages" (and "Edit messages of others", "Delete messages"). Save.
   - TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID in Netlify must be the channel's ID: a number starting with -100.
     To find it: forward any post from Mr.FixitB to @userinfobot (or @JsonDumpBot) in Telegram.
   - Keep the channel PRIVATE: everyone in it sees customer names, phone numbers and amounts.
   - To also use the bot in a private chat, message the bot: it replies with your Telegram ID.
     Add that ID to TELEGRAM_ALLOWED_IDS in Netlify.

1. Make up a secret (16 or more letters, numbers, - or _), for example:   mbs-bot-7Gq2xL9pR4tW8kD
   Keep it private, like a password.

2. Netlify > your site > Site configuration > Environment variables > Add a variable:
      TELEGRAM_WEBHOOK_SECRET = (your secret)
   (TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN and TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID are already there.)

3. Push this version to GitHub (or netlify.cmd deploy --prod) and wait until the deploy says Published.

4. Open this address once in your browser (your site address, then your secret):
      https://mbsplc.com.au/.netlify/functions/telegram?setup=YOUR-SECRET
   It should say "Telegram bot connected", and the bot sends you "MBS job tracker is connected".

5. In Mr.FixitB post /help. Everything works in the channel: alerts with buttons, commands, reminders and the
   Monday report. (Telegram's ☰ Menu command list shows in private chats with the bot, not inside channels;
   in the channel, /help shows the full list.)

Numbers:  new enquiry PW-261025-000012  ->  quoted W-261025-000012  ->  job won JW-261025-000012
          (the middle letter is the trade; short form: /called 12 is the same job)

Good to know
- Reminders are checked every 5 minutes, so they can arrive up to 5 minutes after the set time.
- Monday 7am you get last week's report. To stop it: WEEKLY_REPORT = off in Netlify.
- Staff later: they message the bot, it tells them their Telegram ID; add it to TELEGRAM_ALLOWED_IDS
  (comma separated) in Netlify and redeploy.
- If the same bot is also used by another program that "polls" Telegram (like the job-search bot on your PC),
  that program will stop receiving messages once this is connected. Use a separate bot for each.
- Website previews on your PC don't use the tracker; it only works on the live Netlify site.
