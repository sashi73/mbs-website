MBS website v51 (2026-10-30)
SQL wizard and Ask AI: every table starts with its own key (primary key), named after it: customer_id, job_id, quote_id, invoice_id, stock_id, supplier_id, subcontractor_id, task_id and more; a column pointing at another table has that table's key name (foreign key), e.g. inventory.supplier_id = suppliers.supplier_id, so joins and unions line up. Keys show as 🔑 and links as 🔗 in the table list. Old column names (id, core, item_id) still work in saved reports. Stock items carry who to order from: supplier name, contact, phone, email, terms and how many to re-order. "Short supply", "running low", "order from" now reach the stock table. If a query with a JOIN runs but a column is empty in every row, the AI is asked once to fix it.
v50: Ask AI handles more kinds of sub-contractor questions: two new tables, sub_trades (all 33 trades, with or without subbies: counts, preferred, compliant, average rating and rates) and plan_stakeholders (who is on each project, RACI, which subbie), days since a subbie was last used, and better recognition of everyday words (sparky, roof guy, drain, rates, ratings, papers, "who can", "haven't used").
v49: Ask AI and the SQL wizard know the sub-contractors: a subcontractors table with trade, suburb, region (Western Sydney, Hills District, Blacktown...), areas covered, status and preference, rating, rates, licence, public liability and workers comp with expiry and state (ok, due, expired, missing), and the problems. E.g. "List me all cleaners in Western Sydney sorted by preference, show licence and insurance and flag expired". Expired papers show as ⚠ EXPIRED in red. Phone numbers, emails and contact names never go to the AI.
v48: 50 test sub-contractors.
v47: a new Sub-contractors group in the menu. Subbies: a register of the trades MBS hires in (33 trade categories: plumber, electrician, gas fitter, air conditioning, carpenter, tiler, waterproofer, plasterer, painter, roofer, concreter, cleaner, rubbish removal, asbestos removal and more) with contact details, ABN and GST, NSW licence, public liability, workers comp (or sole trader), white card, SWMS, hourly, call-out and after-hours rates, payment terms, areas covered, availability, rating, status (Preferred, Approved, On trial, On hold, Do not use), last used and notes. Licences & insurance: what is expired, due within 30 days or missing, with call and email buttons; the menu shows how many need following up. Project plan Stakeholders: "Add a subbie" adds one as Responsible. Test data: 30 made-up subbies (deploy\load-test-data.ps1 -What subbies).
