🧱 Bill of Materials (a new BOM tab on each project, after the Board). Start it and the 📦 materials already on the tasks become its first lines. ✨ Draft with AI: the AI reads the plan, the scope and the stock catalogue and suggests the materials, fixings and consumables per task with quantities, waste allowance, Sydney trade prices and lead times, using our stock items where they match; every AI line is marked ≈ est with a warning until it is checked.
