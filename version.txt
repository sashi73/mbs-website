MBS website v59 (2026-11-01)
Project Intelligence: the answers are easier to keep tidy. The newest 2 answers stay open and older ones fold to one line with a short preview (▸ opens, ▾ folds; "Fold all" / "Open all"). ✕ on an answer clears just that one; "🧹 Keep newest 3" clears the older ones; "🗑 Clear all (n)" clears the whole log. Clearing is for everyone on the project and asks first; documents, risks, stakeholders, meetings and the SWMS stay.
v58: Project plan: ⬇ Gantt export (next to ⬇ Excel) exports only the schedule (no costs or other tabs) for other planning tools. Formats: CSV (ID, WBS, outline level, task, start, finish, duration, predecessors, milestone, % complete, status, resources; dates DD/MM/YYYY, YYYY-MM-DD or MM/DD/YYYY) for Smartsheet, Asana, Monday, ClickUp, Wrike, Jira and Excel; Microsoft Project XML (.xml, with links, summary tasks, milestones and a working-day calendar) for Microsoft Project, Primavera P6, ProjectLibre and GanttProject; Excel (.xlsx) with real dates and grouped sub-tasks; and a calendar (.ics) for Google or Outlook calendar. Sub-tasks can be included or left out; a "How to import" guide is in the dialog.
v57: Project plan: a 🦺 SWMS tab (before Project Intelligence). The AI writes the Safe Work Method Statement from the plan (WHS Regulation 2017 NSW, Part 6.3): business and project details, the high-risk construction work categories that apply, job steps with hazards, risk before and after, control measures and who is responsible, PPE, plant, licences, emergency arrangements, implementation and review, consultation and a sign-on sheet. It opens in its own editor (like a quote) where everything can be changed (saves as you type, Draft or Issued, versions), and downloads as Word (.docx) or PDF, or prints. Project Intelligence: "Generate a SWMS for this project" writes it too, and questions about the SWMS are answered from it.
v56: Project plan fits a 14" laptop (screens up to 1680 wide or 920 tall): smaller text, ~35 px rows, slim summary cards, tabs and controls on fewer lines; "+ sub-task" shows when you point at a task. ✨ AI plan: up to 10,000 tokens (AI_PLAN_MAX_TOKENS 2,000-32,000), descriptions up to 6,000 characters, up to 150 tasks, and big projects are planned through every phase with a ✓ milestone at the end of each phase; waits up to 10 minutes; if the reply is cut off the tasks that came back are kept with a warning. AI calls switch to deepseek-flash if "deepseek-chat" is refused, and to the largest length allowed. Materials: a task can use any number of stock items, each with its own quantity (e.g. 4 × Paint 10L); the task cost adds them up, stock is set aside while open and used when Done; older single-item tasks convert with the same cost; quote lines list the materials.
v55: Console menu folds into a slim strip of icons (names show as tooltips) to give the page more room: tap the ‹ tab on its edge, drag the tab left (a slider), swipe the menu left on a touch screen, or press [. Bring it back the same way (› tab, drag right, swipe right, [). Remembered on this computer. Phones keep the ☰ slide-in menu.
v54: Project plan, 📜 Contracts made plain: one line per contract (Customer contract, Sub-contractor from the job's Stakeholders list, Supply, Consultant), upload the signed contract as Word (.docx) or PDF only; download, delete, and 🧠 Ask to have Project Intelligence read it. The contract builder (sums, variations, claims, ✨ fill-in) is removed: ask Project Intelligence about what is in the contracts. Ask AI has a simple plan_contracts list; the Excel export lists the contracts.
v53 (replaced by v54): Project plan: a 📜 Contracts tab (contract and vendor management) between Stakeholders and Project Intelligence.
v52: Project plan: a 🧠 Project Intelligence tab after Stakeholders. Ask anything about the project: the AI (DeepSeek) answers from the whole plan (tasks, dates, critical path, costs, earned value, risks, issues, stakeholders, meetings) and from documents uploaded to the project (contract, specification, quotes, variations, letters; PDF, Word .docx or text, read in the browser with PDF.js and Mammoth; long documents: the most relevant parts are sent). It can work out critical and non-critical risks (one click adds them to the Risk register), draft a stakeholder register (one click adds new people to Stakeholders), and set up a team meeting: Google Calendar opens with the date, time, 45 minutes, guests and agenda and adds the Google Meet link; also an .ics invite, an email to the guests, and a Meetings list on the project. Answers stay with the project for the team. The customer's name, phone and email and stakeholders' phone numbers and emails are taken out before anything goes to the AI. Runs in the background function (no 10-second limit).
v51: SQL wizard and Ask AI: every table starts with its own key (primary key), named after it: customer_id, job_id, quote_id, invoice_id, stock_id, supplier_id, subcontractor_id, task_id and more; a column pointing at another table has that table's key name (foreign key), e.g. inventory.supplier_id = suppliers.supplier_id, so joins and unions line up. Keys show as 🔑 and links as 🔗 in the table list. Old column names (id, core, item_id) still work in saved reports. Stock items carry who to order from: supplier name, contact, phone, email, terms and how many to re-order. "Short supply", "running low", "order from" now reach the stock table. If a query with a JOIN runs but a column is empty in every row, the AI is asked once to fix it.
v50: Ask AI handles more kinds of sub-contractor questions: two new tables, sub_trades (all 33 trades, with or without subbies: counts, preferred, compliant, average rating and rates) and plan_stakeholders (who is on each project, RACI, which subbie), days since a subbie was last used, and better recognition of everyday words (sparky, roof guy, drain, rates, ratings, papers, "who can", "haven't used").
v49: Ask AI and the SQL wizard know the sub-contractors: a subcontractors table with trade, suburb, region (Western Sydney, Hills District, Blacktown...), areas covered, status and preference, rating, rates, licence, public liability and workers comp with expiry and state (ok, due, expired, missing), and the problems. E.g. "List me all cleaners in Western Sydney sorted by preference, show licence and insurance and flag expired". Expired papers show as ⚠ EXPIRED in red. Phone numbers, emails and contact names never go to the AI.
