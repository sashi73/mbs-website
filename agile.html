Choose your way

Three ways to deliver.

📐

Waterfall

Best when scope, budget and dates are agreed up front.

  • Charter and scope sign-off
  • Gantt with dependencies and critical path
  • Baseline, budget and earned value
  • Change requests before scope moves
🔄

Agile

Best when the work is discovered as you go.

  • Backlog of stories with owners
  • Kanban board: To do, Doing, Done
  • Short cycles and quick re-planning
  • Progress rolls up automatically
🧬

Hybrid

Best for most real projects.

  • Gantt for phases and milestones
  • Board for the day-to-day work
  • One set of tasks, two views
  • Sponsors and team stay in sync
